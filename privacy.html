1. Who We Are

Rise And Thrive LLC. ("Rise And Thrive AI," "we," "us," or "our") builds AI-powered voice and SMS agents ("AI Employees") that answer calls, follow up with leads, and book appointments on behalf of our business clients. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website, book a consultation with us, or become a client, and how information is handled when our AI Employees interact with your customers on your behalf.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Website Visitors

  • Basic usage data (pages viewed, browser type, device type, approximate location) collected automatically when you visit our site.
  • Information you submit through our contact widget or booking flow, such as your name and email address.

2.2 Prospective and Existing Clients

  • Contact details you provide when booking a free consultation (name, email, phone number, business name).
  • Business details shared during discovery calls, such as your calendar setup, phone number, scheduling workflow, and services offered.
  • Billing and payment information when you engage our services (processed by our third-party payment processor; we do not store full card details ourselves).

2.3 End Customers Contacted on a Client's Behalf

When you engage Rise And Thrive AI, our AI Employees may call or text your leads and customers ("End Customers") to answer questions, follow up, and book appointments. In this relationship, you (our client) are the data controller for your End Customers' information, and Rise And Thrive AI acts as a service provider / data processor on your behalf. This may include:

  • Phone numbers and names of leads or customers you provide to us, or that are captured through inbound calls/texts.
  • Call audio and transcripts, and SMS message content, generated during interactions between your End Customers and our AI Employees.
  • Appointment details booked into your connected Google Calendar or Calendly account.

It is the client's responsibility to ensure they have the appropriate legal basis and consent to share End Customer contact information with us and to have that information contacted by an AI system, including compliance with call-recording consent laws in their state or jurisdiction.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our website and AI Employee services.
  • To build, configure, and run the specific AI voice/SMS agent(s) a client has engaged us to build.
  • To schedule and manage consultations, demos, and appointments.
  • To bill for services and communicate about your account.
  • To monitor and refine AI Employee performance as part of our ongoing care service.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use End Customer call or text content to train third-party general-purpose AI models beyond what is strictly necessary to deliver the contracted service to our client.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on the following categories of third-party providers to deliver our services. Each processes data under their own privacy terms and security practices:

  • Telephony & SMS: Twilio, for placing/receiving calls and text messages.
  • Voice AI: Vapi, for powering conversational voice agents.
  • Scheduling: Google Calendar and/or Calendly, for booking and managing appointments.
  • Automation: Workflow tools (such as n8n) that connect the above services together.
  • Hosting & Website Analytics: Our website host and any analytics tools we use to understand site traffic.
  • Payments: A third-party payment processor for billing setup fees and monthly retainers.

5. Call & SMS Recording, Transcription, and AI Processing

Calls and text conversations handled by our AI Employees may be recorded, transcribed, and processed by AI systems in order to generate responses, extract appointment details, and allow our team to monitor and improve performance. Where required by law, appropriate notice or consent to recording must be provided to call participants — for clients operating in two-party consent states, this is the client's responsibility to configure and disclose, and we can advise on standard approaches but cannot provide legal guarantees of compliance.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share information:

  • With the third-party service providers listed above, solely to deliver the service.
  • With our client, when the information relates to that client's own End Customers or account.
  • If required by law, subpoena, or legal process.
  • In connection with a business transfer, merger, or acquisition of Rise And Thrive LLC.

7. Data Retention

We retain client account information for as long as the business relationship continues and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, billing, and record-keeping purposes. Call recordings, transcripts, and SMS logs are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to deliver and support the service, after which they may be deleted or anonymized, unless a longer period is required by law or requested by the client.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. To make a request, contact us at hello@risethriveai.com. If you are an End Customer of one of our clients and wish to exercise a data right, we may direct your request to the relevant client, as they control that relationship.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and individuals over the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
